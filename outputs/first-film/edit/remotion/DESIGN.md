# The Last Firework

## Style Prompt
A naturalistic English first-person campfire horror story. Begin with real summer warmth, move through purple-orange twilight and into a readable flashlight-lit forest. Framing, restraint and pauses carry the dread; the film does not look like an animated interface.

## Colors

- #101A17 — forest-black canvas
- #F2EBDF — warm ivory title
- #C78956 — ember accent
- #465B62 — restrained night shadow

## Typography

Barlow Condensed 900 for the film title. Spectral 300 for the short subtitle. No captions baked into the image assets; optional English subtitles remain a separate deliverable.

## Motion

Small deliberate push-ins, shallow pans and purposeful holds. Crossfades for memories and elapsed time; direct cuts for reactions and revelation. No repeated short animation and no stock whoosh on every cut.

## What NOT to Do

- No constant blue fog or darkness during the happy opening.
- No neon, HUD, bouncing titles or TikTok word highlighting.
- No crushed blacks hiding important clues.
- No early reveal of creatures or the blue fabric clue.
- No claims that camera drift is generated actor movement.
